# Mizan — Product Vision & Future Direction

## Status
**State:** Product direction established — detailed business and domain decisions remain subject to their respective design gates.
**Phase:** M0 — Product & Domain Foundation

## 1. Vision
Mizan is intended to become a **Personal Financial Operating System**: a trusted financial companion that helps people capture, understand, control, and improve their financial lives.

It is not intended to be merely an expense tracker, a collection of charts, a generic AI chatbot, or a country-specific bookkeeping application.

The long-term goal is to make personal financial management dramatically easier, more accurate, more understandable, and more actionable, with enough recurring value that users willingly pay a subscription.

## 2. The problem
People need more than transaction recording. They need answers to:
- What is my real financial position?
- Where did my money go?
- What changed?
- Which payments are actually my expenses?
- Which amounts should I recover from others?
- What commitments are coming?
- What can I safely afford?
- Why did my balance change?
- What deserves my attention?
- Can I trust the system enough to make decisions from it?

Mizan therefore treats **financial truth, financial understanding, and financial actionability** as connected product layers.

## 3. Product promise
Mizan should progressively provide:

**Capture → Understand → Explain → Predict → Recommend → Act**

with a strict boundary:

> Authoritative domain data and explicit financial rules are the financial source of truth. AI may interpret, assist, predict, recommend, and automate approved workflows, but AI must not silently become financial truth.

## 4. Core product pillars

### Frictionless capture
Support evolution toward manual input, receipt/image input, voice input, bank notifications/messages, imports, integrations, and other evidence channels. All inputs normalize through the same authoritative validation and domain rules.

### Economic reality
A payment is not automatically a personal expense. The product should eventually distinguish personal expenses, income, transfers, payments on behalf of others, reimbursements/receivables, advances, shared expenses, obligations, and informational events with no balance effect. Exact semantics belong to domain gates.

### Trusted financial truth
Every important balance and historical state should be explainable from authoritative records. Caches, analytics, AI interpretations, and UI state must not silently override financial truth.

### AI-native intelligence
AI should become a controlled intelligence layer over trusted financial data: natural-language input, receipt extraction, voice understanding, classification, anomaly detection, explanations, forecasting, affordability analysis, scenarios, recommendations, personalization, and approved automation.

### Financial copilot
Mizan should progressively become proactive: explain changes, surface unusual activity, identify upcoming commitments, detect unrecovered amounts, and highlight decisions that deserve attention.

### One financial inbox
Future flow:
**Evidence/Input → Extraction → Candidate Event → Validation → Confirmation/Policy → Authoritative Record → Intelligence**

### Global by design
The product may start in Egypt and be initially used personally, but the core must not be customized around Egypt, Arabic, one provider, or one person's habits. Localization and market-specific capabilities should extend rather than fork the financial core.

## 5. Competitive ambition
The objective is not to copy large finance applications feature-for-feature.

The intended differentiation is the combination of:
1. low-friction capture;
2. richer economic semantics;
3. trusted and explainable financial truth;
4. AI-native understanding;
5. proactive financial intelligence;
6. user-controlled automation;
7. global extensibility;
8. privacy and data ownership;
9. one coherent financial experience.

The durable advantage should increasingly come from the quality of the user's financial model, history, personalization, trust, intelligence, and workflows.

## 6. Subscription value hypothesis
Users should pay because Mizan repeatedly creates measurable value:
- less financial administration;
- easier capture;
- better visibility;
- fewer missed obligations;
- faster understanding of anomalies;
- better cash-flow planning;
- personalized insights;
- scenario analysis;
- intelligent automation.

Free/basic capabilities may demonstrate trust and value; paid tiers should unlock substantial recurring intelligence and automation. Pricing and packaging remain hypotheses to validate.

## 7. Future capability map
- **Financial Core:** accounts, financial events, balances, history, corrections, currencies, ownership, auditability.
- **Capture Layer:** manual, receipt intelligence, voice, notifications, imports, integrations.
- **Understanding Layer:** categories, merchants/counterparties, recurring items, obligations, reimbursements, commitments, cash flow, net position.
- **Intelligence Layer:** explanations, anomaly detection, forecasting, affordability, scenarios, recommendations.
- **Action Layer:** rules, reminders, plans, approved automations, workflows.
- **Experience Layer:** mobile-first UI, conversational assistance, proactive notifications, timeline, search, dashboards, financial inbox.
- **Expansion Layer:** family/shared finance, freelancer finance, small business, multi-currency, international integrations, partner ecosystem, market-specific capabilities.

These are strategic capability areas, not an MVP commitment.

## 8. Evolution principle
Build the smallest trustworthy foundation that preserves the ability to grow into the larger vision.

Separate:
- financial truth from derived intelligence;
- domain semantics from input channels;
- core rules from localization;
- user data from AI models;
- capabilities from specific providers;
- current release scope from future boundaries.

## 9. North-star outcome
The long-term success question is:

**Can Mizan reliably help a person understand and improve their financial position with dramatically less effort than managing it alone?**

This guides product decisions while remaining subject to user evidence, market validation, and financial-domain correctness.
