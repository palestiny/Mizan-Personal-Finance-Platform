## Product thesis
Mizan should compete as a **Personal Financial Operating System**, not another expense tracker.

The first product experience must prove that Mizan can:
1. represent financial truth correctly;
2. make capture materially easier;
3. explain financial state usefully;
4. create recurring value that can justify payment.

## AI boundary
AI may perform:
- natural-language understanding;
- extraction;
- classification;
- ambiguity resolution;
- summarization;
- anomaly detection;
- forecasting assistance;
- scenario generation;
- personalized explanations;
- recommendation generation;
- workflow preparation;
- approved automation.

AI must not silently determine authoritative balances, historical truth, irreversible financial facts, or consequential actions outside explicit policy.

## Commercial boundary
The accepted direction is a trust-first freemium/subscription hypothesis. Pricing, packaging, exact free/paid limits, and willingness-to-pay remain validation questions and are not treated as settled pricing decisions.

User financial data is not to be treated as the commodity funding the business.

## Strategic expansion rule
The roadmap is a staged strategy, not a commitment to build every future capability before validating the previous stage.

Expansion requires evidence.
